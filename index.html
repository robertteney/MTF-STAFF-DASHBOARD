OUR PURPOSE

Helping students move forward, one certification at a time.

MTF staff support graduates as they prepare for certification exams, overcome challenges, and work toward their career goals. Our role is to provide clear guidance, encouragement, accountability, and access to the right resources so students can make informed decisions and stay engaged throughout their certification journey.

Through personalized one-on-one assistance, targeted boot camps, exam scheduling guidance, timely follow-ups, and accurate progress tracking, we help students identify areas for improvement, build confidence, and take meaningful next steps—even after an unsuccessful exam attempt.

Our commitment: Every student deserves professional, consistent, and supportive communication focused on progress, preparation, and long-term success.

What is the MTF?

The MTF (Maximum Time Frame) period is a designated timeframe after a student graduates to continue preparing for and taking their certification exams!

MTF Voucher Limitations

Please keep the following critical policy limitations in mind:

  • Students can only have ONE active voucher at a time.
  • Students are approved for up to a 4th attempt at an exam.
  • Any certification voucher requested must be completed by the student's designated MTF expiration date.
